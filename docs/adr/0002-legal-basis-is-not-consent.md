# Legal basis for Caretaker data is the volunteering relationship, not consent

The data controller is the animal-welfare association. Caretaker personal data (name, email, phone) is processed under GDPR art. 6.1.b, the volunteering relationship (Ley 45/2015 de Voluntariado), not under consent (art. 6.1.a). So there is no "I consent" checkbox, and stopping volunteering triggers anonymisation (ADR-0001) rather than a consent withdrawal. When accepting an Invitation, the Caretaker is shown the art. 13 privacy notice, and we record the date and the version of that notice. If the notice changes, it is shown again at the next sign-in.

## Consequences

If the system is ever run by a city council instead of an association, the basis becomes art. 6.1.e (public interest, Ley 7/2023) and the privacy notice must change accordingly.
